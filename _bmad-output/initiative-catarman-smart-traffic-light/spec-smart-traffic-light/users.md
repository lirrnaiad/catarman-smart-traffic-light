# Users

## Primary: municipal operations (dashboard operators)

- **POSO (Public Order and Safety Office).** Field enforcers on Catarman commercial corridors: the Public Market, Jacinto St. and the UEP access corridors.
  - *Pains:* heat and storm-flood hazard; confrontations during roadside apprehensions; no data on peak bottlenecks.
  - *Goals:* automated intersection control, zero storm standing hours, objective digital audit logs (CAP-4, CAP-6, CAP-9).
- **MDRRMO (Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office).** Dispatchers coordinating convoys and ambulances during monsoons and typhoons.
  - *Pains:* convoys stuck at unmanaged flooded junctions; no street-level flood telemetry.
  - *Goals:* clear evacuation corridors (manual override, CAP-5) and real-time flood detection (CAP-3, CAP-18).

## Secondary

- **TODA operators and provincial motorists.** Daily-wage drivers whose income depends on turnaround time.
  - *Pains:* idling at empty cross-streets burns fuel; chaotic gridlock at un-signaled junctions.
  - *Goals:* responsive greens (CAP-14) and predictable countdowns. The controller-box OLED shows a countdown and the dashboard shows it too (M2, CAP-8); no road-facing one is planned.
- **Municipal IT and engineering technicians.**
  - *Pains:* cannot service proprietary DPWH controllers that need Manila technicians.
  - *Goals:* modular, open-source hardware they can repair and reconfigure in-house.

## Audiences

- **M1:** the course instructor (Application Development & Emerging Technologies).
- **M2:** the pitch judges are the MDRRMO Head, an EE Professor, a DICT Representative and the University ICT Director. They are assumed to weight climate survivability and fail-safe degradation above cloud features.

## Stakeholder input

- **POSO:** enforcers abandon posts in severe downpours, which leaves intersections unregulated.
- **MDRRMO:** gridlock at flooded junctions blocks evacuation convoys.
- **TODA Federation:** compliance improves when signals remove "ghost red" idling and show countdown clocks.
