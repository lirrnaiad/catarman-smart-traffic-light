Status keyReply due in 5 s

  • Requested

    Sent. Waiting for the controller.

  • Applied

    Controller accepted it. Final.

  • Rejected

    Refused, with a reason. Final.

  • Timeout

    No reply in 5 s. Not final: a late reply still resolves it.

Rejection reasons

CONTROLLER_OFFLINE

Decided by the backend before sending
CONFLICT_FAULT
Corridor refused during a conflict fault

Active vs requested

An override is active only once the controller reports it. Until then the hazard strip keeps showing the last mode it reported. One override at a time: a new command replaces the current one.
