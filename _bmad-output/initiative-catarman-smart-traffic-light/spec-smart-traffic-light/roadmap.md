# Roadmap (beyond M2; non-goals for this spec)

## Phase 2: post-event municipal hardening

- **Industrial hardware and weatherproofing:** DIN-rail industrial ESP32 controllers (e.g. Norvi or KinCony PLCs) in NEMA 4X / IP66 pole enclosures, with IP67 JSN-SR04T ultrasonic sensors and stainless-steel float switches.
- **220 V AC SSR integration:** optically isolated high-voltage switching for standard 220 V AC, 50 W municipal LED signal heads.
- **Homography speed estimation:** camera perspective matrices for reliable speed warnings on school approaches.
- **TODA franchise audit registry:** a batch-export bridge from violation logs to the Catarman Municipal Licensing Office, gated on Tier 2 (`privacy-compliance.md`).
- **Solar-battery islanding:** a 100 W PV panel, 12 V LiFePO4 pack and MPPT controller for multi-day off-grid operation.

## Long term (1–2 years)

- **Arterial green wave:** ESP-NOW or LoRaWAN mesh between adjacent intersections on Jacinto St. and UEP road, with no cellular link or fiber.
- **MDRRMO river basin link:** connect to the Catarman River Flood Early Warning System (FEWS) to lock high-ground evacuation routes automatically during river swelling.
