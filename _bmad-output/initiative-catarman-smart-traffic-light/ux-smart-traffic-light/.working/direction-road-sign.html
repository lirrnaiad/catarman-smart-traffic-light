Road Sign

The dashboard speaks the grammar drivers in Catarman already obey: asphalt, white lane markings, and sign plates whose shape and color name the category before a word is read. Painted plates are flat and never glow (green guide plate = information and flow, yellow diamond = caution and flood, red plate or octagon = MDRRMO authority and breaches), while real signal aspects appear only as lamps lit inside black signal housings, so a green plate can never be read as a green light. Wide uppercase highway lettering and very large numerals keep every value legible from 3 to 4 m.

Sign key · how color is read
Lit lamp in a black housingThe only place red, yellow and green glow. Always a signal aspect, always with a text label.
Green guide plateInformation and flow: level L1, vehicle counts. Flat, never a lamp.
Yellow warning plateCaution: flood sensor, L3, L4. Diamond means a hazard.
Red regulatory plate or octagonMDRRMO authority, breaches and FAULT. Octagon marks a stop-type action.
White plateNeutral data: PCU, countdown, link health, L2.
White pavement markingStructure only: dividers and lanes. Never yellow.
