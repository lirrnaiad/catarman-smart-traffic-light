The operator screen is built from the thing it controls: matte charcoal housings, visored round lenses, riveted stencil plates, and yellow-and-black hazard paint around anything that takes INT-01 out of automatic. Colour discipline: a glowing round lens always means a live signal aspect and always sits beside its text label. Decorative red, yellow and green only appear as unlit tinted glass (the brand mark). Alert and override zones use matte hazard stripes that are never round and never glow, and link health uses square cyan pilots. Weighty, physical, trustworthy, at medium density.
